PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: September 28, 2026
01 —
Introduction

Welcome to DevShowcase, a global platform built by BraxCode Digitals Foundation, based in Mwanza, Tanzania 🇹🇿.

We are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over your information.

By using DevShowcase, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02 —
Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use DevShowcase:

  • Account Information — Your name, email address, and password when you sign up, or the name, email address and profile picture we receive from Google or GitHub if you sign in with them
  • Profile Information — Your role, bio, skills, WhatsApp number, GitHub, LinkedIn, and website links
  • Location Data — Your approximate location (city and country), and the coordinates it was derived from, detected via GPS (with your permission) or IP address. Coordinates are stored privately and are never shown publicly
  • Project Data — Project names, descriptions, screenshots, tech stack, and live links you upload
  • Usage Data — Reactions, comments, and interactions on the platform
  • Device Information — Browser type and device type for improving user experience
  • Guest Activity — If you react or comment without an account, we store your reaction or comment and a random anonymous ID saved in your browser
  • Views & Notifications — We record views of your projects and profile so we can show you view counts and notify you
03 —
How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Display your developer profile and projects publicly on the platform
  • Connect you with potential clients who are looking for developers
  • Show location-based results to help clients find developers near them
  • Send important account notifications (email verification, password reset)
  • Improve the platform based on usage patterns
  • Respond to your support requests
  • List your profile in the Developers directory and the project gallery
  • Make your public profile page discoverable by search engines such as Google
  • Notify you when your projects get views, reactions or comments
  • Protect the platform from abuse, for example by rate limiting sign-ins and AI chat requests, which may use your IP address

We do NOT use your information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

04 —
Information We Share

We share your information with the following trusted service providers only:

  • Supabase — Our database and authentication provider. Your data is stored securely on their servers.
  • Groq AI — Powers our AI customer care assistant. Only your chat messages are processed — we do not share personal data.
  • ipapi.co — Used for IP-based location detection when GPS is not available.
  • Google & GitHub — If you choose to sign in with them, they share your name, email and profile picture with us.
  • Vercel — Hosts the website and our server functions, so they process your requests, including your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — Receives your coordinates to convert them into a city and country.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties without your explicit consent.

Some of these providers process data on servers outside Tanzania.

05 —
Location Data

DevShowcase uses your location to help clients find developers in their region.

  • We request GPS permission from your browser when you create an account, or when you sign in with Google or GitHub and have no saved location — you can deny it and the platform still works
  • Your coordinates are converted into a city and country using OpenStreetMap
  • If you sign in with Google or GitHub and deny GPS, we may estimate your location from your IP address
  • Your exact coordinates are never shown publicly — only your country appears on your public profile
  • To change or remove your saved location, contact us
06 —
Data Security

We take security seriously and implement the following measures:

  • All data is stored on Supabase — encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed by Supabase Auth
  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensures you can only access and modify your own data
  • Rate limits on sign-in and AI chat requests help protect against abuse

However, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

07 —
Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Update — Edit your profile and project information at any time from your dashboard
  • Delete — Delete your account yourself from your dashboard (Delete Account), or contact us. Your data is removed within 30 days
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent — Stop using the platform and request data removal at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or email.

08 —
Cookies & Storage

DevShowcase uses the following browser storage:

  • localStorage — To keep an anonymous ID that identifies you as a guest, so your reactions as a guest are linked to your browser. Your AI chat lasts only while the page is open
  • Supabase session — Stored in your browser to keep you signed in to your developer account

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

09 —
Children's Privacy

DevShowcase is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account immediately.

10 —
Public Profile & Search Engines

DevShowcase is a public platform. The following information is visible to anyone, including people without an account:

  • Your name, role, bio, skills, country and profile picture
  • Your projects, screenshots and live links, plus reactions and comments on them
  • Links you add, such as GitHub or your website
  • Your WhatsApp number — visitors can use it to message you directly

Your email address is not shown publicly.

Profile pages can be indexed by search engines like Google, so people may find your profile when they search your name. If you delete your account, we remove your data from DevShowcase, but search engines may keep cached copies for some time.

Only add information you are comfortable sharing publicly. If you do not want direct contact, leave the WhatsApp field empty.

11 —
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of DevShowcase after changes means you accept the updated policy.
