PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: April 25, 2026
01 —
👋 Introduction

Welcome to DevShowcase, a global platform built by BraxCode Digitals Foundation, based in Mwanza, Tanzania 🇹🇿.

We are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over your information.

By using DevShowcase, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02 —
📊 Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use DevShowcase:

  • Account Information — Your name, email address, and password when you sign up
  • Profile Information — Your role, bio, skills, WhatsApp number, GitHub, LinkedIn, and website links
  • Location Data — Your approximate location (city and country) detected via GPS or IP address, used to connect you with nearby clients
  • Project Data — Project names, descriptions, screenshots, tech stack, and live links you upload
  • Usage Data — Reactions, comments, and interactions on the platform
  • Device Information — Browser type and device type for improving user experience
03 —
🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Display your developer profile and projects publicly on the platform
  • Connect you with potential clients who are looking for developers
  • Show location-based results to help clients find developers near them
  • Send important account notifications (email verification, password reset)
  • Improve the platform based on usage patterns
  • Respond to your support requests

We do NOT use your information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

04 —
🤝 Information We Share

We share your information with the following trusted service providers only:

  • Supabase — Our database and authentication provider. Your data is stored securely on their servers.
  • Groq AI — Powers our AI customer care assistant. Only your chat messages are processed — we do not share personal data.
  • ipapi.co — Used for IP-based location detection when GPS is not available.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties without your explicit consent.

05 —
📍 Location Data

DevShowcase uses your location to help clients find developers in their region and to show you relevant local opportunities.

  • We request GPS permission from your browser — you can deny this and the platform still works
  • If GPS is denied, we use your IP address to estimate your location
  • Your exact GPS coordinates are never shown publicly — only your city and country
  • You can update your location from your profile settings at any time
06 —
🔒 Data Security

We take security seriously and implement the following measures:

  • All data is stored on Supabase — encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed by Supabase Auth
  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensures you can only access and modify your own data
  • Admin access is password protected and not accessible to regular users

However, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

07 —
⚖️ Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Update — Edit your profile and project information at any time from your dashboard
  • Delete — Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent — Stop using the platform and request data removal at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or email.

08 —
🍪 Cookies & Storage

DevShowcase uses the following browser storage:

  • localStorage — To remember your reactions on projects and AI chat history
  • sessionStorage — To keep you logged into the admin panel during your session
  • Supabase session cookies — To keep you logged in to your developer account

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

09 —
👶 Children's Privacy

DevShowcase is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account immediately.

10 —
🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of DevShowcase after changes means you accept the updated policy.
